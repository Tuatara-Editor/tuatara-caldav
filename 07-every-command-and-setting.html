Every command and setting

Each command and setting of caldav, to look one up by name.

The tables are made from the program each time this manual is made, so they never list a command or setting that caldav does not have. The first table has four columns. The first two are keys: the modal keys, which work only with modal editing on, and the non-modal keys, which work with it off too. A command with no key says M-x and its name in the second column, because you can only run it by its name. The third column is the name of the command and the fourth says what it does.

To run a command by its name, press M-x, type a few letters of the name and press RET. To find a command from within the editor, press C-h f. To find the command that a key runs, press C-h k and then the key. The editor's own settings and commands are in chapters 21 and 22 of its manual.

7.1 Every command

ModalNon-modalCommandWhat it does
noneC-c s lcaldav-calendarsSay the calendars each account of caldav-accounts has, by the names its :CALENDARS takes, and which of them are synced: a way to see that Tuatara reaches the server, and what it finds there.
noneC-c s dcaldav-deletedDelete on their servers the events whose entries were deleted from the calendars' file, after asking; or, answered no, put their entries back as the server has them.
noneC-c s ncaldav-event-newWrite a new event for one of your calendars: choose the calendar (when there is more than one), then give its title, its place (RET for none) and its time in the calendar.
noneC-c s ecaldav-exportWrite the entry at point and those under it, or the entries selected, as a calendar file (.ics) other calendars read: their times, title, place, tags and description.
noneC-c s icaldav-importRead a calendar file (.ics) into the calendars' file: under one of your calendars, whose server gets its events at the next sync, or under a heading of its own, never synced.
noneC-c s scaldav-syncSync the calendars of your Calendaring Extensions to WebDAV (CalDAV) accounts (caldav-accounts) with the calendars' Org file (caldav-file), a heading each: what changed in an entry here is sent, what changed on the server is written into its entry, your own lines in it kept, and new events are added under their calendar.

7.2 Every setting

SettingDefaultValuesWhat it changes
*caldav-accounts*nila list, each (:NAME NAME :URL URL :USER USER :CALENDARS (NAME …))The accounts whose calendars are synced.
*caldav-file*"agenda-caldav.org"a file's nameThe Org file the synced calendars are kept in.
*caldav-minutes*1nil, or a number of minutes, or a time in words such as 500ms, 90s, 30m or 2h, at most a hundred yearsHow often the calendars are synced on their own, in minutes.
*caldav-subscriptions*nila list, each (:NAME NAME :URL URL)Calendar files at an address, read into the agenda and never sent back.
