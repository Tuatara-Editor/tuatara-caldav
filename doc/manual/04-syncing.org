#+title: Syncing
#+language: en

* Syncing

When calendars are synced, and what each command does.

** Automatic syncing

The calendars sync on their own, a few seconds after Tuatara starts and then
every minute, in the background. Nothing is said unless something changed or
failed. A failure is told once, not again every minute while it lasts.

A sync that finds nothing changed costs one request for each account's
calendars, and reads and writes nothing on your drive.

=*caldav-minutes*= sets how often. Give minutes, or words such as ="30m"= or
="1h"=. It is never more often than once a minute. Give =nil=, or 0 or less, to
sync only when asked.

#+begin_src lisp
(setf *caldav-minutes* 5)
#+end_src

** Syncing at once

~C-c s s~ (=caldav-sync=) syncs every account's calendars and fetches the
subscriptions, in the background. The first sync writes =agenda-caldav.org=
beside your agenda file, a heading for each calendar, and the agenda shows its
events. In an Org file or the agenda ~C-c s s~ works. =M-x caldav-sync= works
anywhere.

A sync sends what you changed in an entry and fetches what changed on the server
since the last sync, then writes it into its entry. Only the lines that differ
are changed, as one step to undo, and the file is saved. Your own lines in an
entry (notes, checkboxes, clocks, properties of your own) are kept when the
server's version is written. When the file is open with changes you have not
saved, the sync writes beside them and leaves the saving to you; its line says
so. A sync on its own never asks a question. While a sync runs, ~C-c s s~ says
so and starts no other.

After each sync Tuatara says what it did in one line, and what failed after
=failed:=. ~C-h e~ shows the messages again. A failed sync is also told as a
notification, on the desktop and in =*Notifications*= (~C-h N~).

** What happens to an entry

- New entries under a calendar's heading become events on its server. Their UID
  is written into them.
- Deleted on the server: the entry is deleted too. One that holds lines of yours
  (notes, checkboxes, clocks, properties of your own) stays. It is tagged
  =removed= and no longer synced, and its times are made inactive
  (=[2026-11-05 Thu]=) so the agenda shows it no more. Delete it when you are
  done with it.
- Deleted here: nothing happens on the server until ~C-c s d~ asks you. When you
  answer no, the entries are put back.
- Cancelled here (~C-c X~, keyword =CANCELLED= or =CANCELED=): its event is
  deleted on the server at the next sync, so it leaves your other devices. The
  entry stays, tagged =removed= and no longer synced. The agenda still shows it,
  cancelled. An entry cancelled before it was ever sent is not sent.
- Changed on both sides: the server's version stands in the entry, and yours is
  kept beside it, tagged =conflict= and no longer synced. Copy what you want
  from it and delete it.

** The other commands

~C-c s l~ (=caldav-calendars=) says the calendars each account has and which of
them are synced. It only asks the server, so it shows that Tuatara reaches it.
It works when nothing else does.

~C-c s d~ (=caldav-deleted=) deletes on the server the events whose entries you
deleted, after asking. Answer no and the entries are put back, written in the
background.

~C-c s i~ (=caldav-import=) reads a calendar file (=.ics=) under one of your
calendars, or under a heading of its own.

~C-c s e~ (=caldav-export=) writes the entry at point and those under it, or the
entries selected, as a calendar file, in the background. It is bound in Org
files only.

** A new event

~C-c s n~ (=caldav-event-new=, or =M-x caldav-event-new= anywhere) asks which
calendar when you have more than one, then the event's title, its place (~RET~
for none) and its time in the calendar. A form opens with the cursor between the
description block's two lines. Write the description there, or nothing.

- ~C-c C-c~ files the event under the calendar's heading, and the server gets it
  at the next sync.
- ~C-c C-w~ files it under another heading instead.
- ~C-c C-k~ throws it away.

The =#+name: description= line marks the block as the description, so leave it
as it is. A location or description left blank is none, and nothing is sent for
it.
